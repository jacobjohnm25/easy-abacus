Sketch

Draw anything freehand: a straight stroke becomes a board, an L or zig-zag becomes joined boards, a smooth curve becomes a curved piece, and a closed shape (box, circle or any outline) becomes a sheet piece. Then tap a piece and type its real size, like 34, 2' 10" or 16 x 24; pieces you haven't sized are scaled from the ones you have. Line and Rectangle draw exact, grid-snapped pieces (drag or tap two points). With the sketch focused: [ and ] pick a piece, Enter types its size, arrow keys move it, Delete removes it.
